Find Where Any Photo Was Taken

Every time you take a photo with your smartphone (with location services enabled), the device embeds GPS coordinates into the photo's EXIF metadata. These coordinates record the exact latitude and longitude where you pressed the shutter button.

Our Find Photo Location tool reads these embedded coordinates and shows you the location on an interactive map. You can zoom in to street level, switch between map and satellite views, and get the full address.

Preguntas Frecuentes

How does Find Photo Location work?

The tool reads the EXIF metadata stored inside the image file and extracts the GPS latitude and longitude. It then converts these coordinates into a point on an interactive map. All processing happens in your browser — the photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Why doesn't my photo show a location?

The photo may not contain GPS data. This happens when: the camera or phone didn't have location services enabled, the EXIF metadata was stripped by a social media platform, or the photo was taken with a camera without GPS. Screenshots also don't have GPS data.

Can I find the location of any photo from the internet?

Only if the photo still has its GPS EXIF data. Most social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) strip EXIF metadata on upload. Photos from stock sites, blogs, and forums may or may not retain EXIF data depending on the platform.

How accurate is the location shown?

The accuracy depends on the GPS signal when the photo was taken. Outdoor photos with clear sky typically have GPS accuracy within 3-5 meters. Indoor photos or areas with weak signal can be off by hundreds of meters.

Can I find location from a screenshot?

No. Screenshots don't contain GPS EXIF data. The operating system creates a new image without GPS information.

Does this work with iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes, the tool supports HEIC/HEIF format. Upload the .heic file directly — no conversion needed.
