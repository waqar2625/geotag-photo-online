About Our Image Converter

Our image converter supports 9 conversion paths between the most common image formats: JPG (JPEG), PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, SVG, and PSD. Every conversion runs entirely in your browser — your images are never uploaded to any server.

Supported Format Conversions

To PNG (Lossless Quality)

Convert JPG to PNG, WebP to PNG, PSD to PNG, and SVG to PNG. PNG uses lossless compression and supports transparency — ideal for graphics, logos, and screenshots.

To JPG (Smaller File Size)

Convert PNG to JPG, WebP to JPG, and HEIC to JPG. JPG uses lossy compression for dramatically smaller file sizes — ideal for photographs and web images where file size matters.

To WebP (Web Optimized)

Convert JPG to WebP and PNG to WebP. WebP is Google's modern image format that delivers 25-50% smaller files than JPG and PNG at equivalent quality. Essential for web performance optimization.

How to Choose the Right Format

  • JPG: Best for photographs. Smallest file sizes. No transparency. Lossy compression.
  • PNG: Best for graphics, logos, screenshots. Supports transparency. Lossless. Larger files.
  • WebP: Best for web delivery. Supports both lossy and lossless. Smallest files for equivalent quality. All modern browsers support it.
  • HEIC: Apple's efficient format for iPhones. Smaller than JPG but not universally supported.
  • SVG: Vector format for logos and icons. Scales to any size without quality loss.
  • PSD: Adobe Photoshop's layered format. Convert to PNG for sharing without Photoshop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All conversions happen entirely in your browser using the Canvas API and JavaScript libraries. Your images never leave your device.

What formats can I convert between?

You can convert between JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, SVG, and PSD. We support 9 conversion paths including JPG to PNG, PNG to JPG, HEIC to JPG, PSD to PNG, SVG to PNG, and conversions to and from WebP.

Is there a file size limit?

There's no hard limit, but very large images (over 50 megapixels) may cause your browser to slow down due to memory constraints. Most photos and graphics convert instantly.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes, batch conversion is supported on all converter pages. Drop up to 20 images and they'll all be converted at once.

Does converting affect image quality?

It depends on the conversion. Converting to a lossless format (PNG) preserves full quality. Converting to a lossy format (JPG, WebP) uses compression that can be adjusted via a quality slider. Converting from lossy to lossless doesn't restore lost data.

Why should I convert images to WebP?

WebP provides 25-50% smaller file sizes than JPG and PNG at equivalent visual quality. All modern browsers support WebP, making it ideal for web performance. Converting your site images to WebP can significantly improve page load speed.
