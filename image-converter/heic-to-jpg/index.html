Why iPhones Use HEIC Instead of JPG

Apple switched the default iPhone photo format from JPG to HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) starting with iOS 11 in 2017. The reason: HEIC files are roughly 50% smaller than equivalent-quality JPGs. For a 128 GB iPhone, this means storing roughly twice as many photos.

HEIC uses the HEVC (H.265) compression codec, which is significantly more efficient than the JPEG codec from 1992. HEIC also supports 16-bit color depth (vs JPG's 8-bit), Live Photos, image sequences, and depth maps from Portrait mode.

The HEIC Compatibility Problem

Despite its technical advantages, HEIC has a major compatibility problem: it's not universally supported. Windows PCs can't open HEIC without installing an extension. Many websites, apps, email clients, and online services don't accept HEIC uploads. Even some photo printing services require JPG.

This creates friction whenever iPhone users need to share photos with non-Apple users, upload to websites, or use photos in professional workflows. Converting HEIC to JPG solves this instantly.

Common Scenarios

Sharing Photos with Windows Users

When you send HEIC photos to someone on Windows, they may see broken file icons or error messages. Converting to JPG before sharing ensures everyone can open the photos regardless of their operating system.

Uploading to Websites and Forms

Many websites, job applications, government forms, and online services only accept JPG, PNG, or PDF uploads. HEIC is not listed as an accepted format. Quick conversion to JPG solves the upload issue.

Photo Editing in Non-Apple Software

While Adobe Lightroom and recent Photoshop versions support HEIC, many other editors (GIMP, Paint.net, Canva, older Photoshop versions) do not. Converting to JPG gives you universal compatibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC format?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is an image format developed by MPEG that Apple adopted as the default photo format on iPhone and iPad since iOS 11 (2017). HEIC files are roughly half the size of equivalent JPGs while maintaining the same visual quality. The format is based on the HEVC (H.265) video codec.

Why can't I open HEIC files on Windows?

Windows doesn't natively support HEIC files. While Microsoft offers a HEVC extension (sometimes paid), many Windows users, apps, and websites can't open .heic files. Converting to JPG makes the photos universally viewable.

Does HEIC to JPG conversion lose quality?

There is minimal quality loss because JPG uses lossy compression. However, at our default quality setting (92%), the difference is virtually imperceptible. HEIC files are already compressed (albeit more efficiently), so the visual difference between HEIC and high-quality JPG is negligible.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Yes, batch conversion is supported. Select multiple .heic or .heif files from your iPhone photo export and convert them all at once.

How do I transfer HEIC photos from iPhone to this tool?

On iPhone: use AirDrop to send to a Mac, email them to yourself, use a USB cable, or save them to Files/iCloud. On the web: if accessing this tool from your iPhone's browser, tap 'Upload' and select photos from your camera roll directly.

Can I make my iPhone save photos as JPG instead of HEIC?

Yes. Go to Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible. This saves photos as JPG directly. The tradeoff is larger file sizes (roughly 2x) on your iPhone storage.

Are my iPhone photos uploaded to a server?

No. The HEIC files are decoded and converted entirely in your browser using the heic2any JavaScript library. No data is transmitted.
