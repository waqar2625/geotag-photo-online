When to Convert SVG to PNG

Social Media & Profile Pictures

Social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn) don't accept SVG uploads. If you have a vector logo or icon, convert it to PNG for profile pictures, post images, and cover photos.

Presentations & Documents

While some presentation software supports SVG, many don't render it reliably (especially older PowerPoint versions). Converting to high-resolution PNG ensures your logos and icons look sharp in any presentation.

E-commerce & Marketplace Listings

Etsy, Amazon, eBay, and most e-commerce platforms require JPEG or PNG product images. Convert vector product illustrations and logos to PNG before uploading.

Favicon & App Icons

While modern browsers support SVG favicons, app stores (iOS, Android, Microsoft) require PNG icons at specific resolutions. Use this tool to export your vector icon at the required sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SVG?

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) is an XML-based vector image format. Unlike raster formats (JPG, PNG), SVG images are defined by mathematical shapes and can scale to any size without losing quality. They're commonly used for logos, icons, illustrations, and web graphics.

Why convert SVG to PNG?

Many platforms don't accept SVG uploads (social media, Etsy, most e-commerce sites). Some email clients don't render SVG. And some contexts require fixed-resolution images. Converting to PNG gives you a compatible raster file.

What resolution should I export?

For web: 2x the display size (e.g., 400x400 for a 200x200 display area). For social media: at least 1200px wide. For print: 300 DPI at the physical size (e.g., 3000x3000 for a 10x10 inch print at 300 DPI).

Is transparency preserved?

Yes. PNG supports full alpha transparency. Transparent areas in the SVG remain transparent in the PNG.

Can complex SVGs be converted?

The converter renders the SVG using the browser's SVG engine, which handles most SVG features including gradients, filters, text, paths, and CSS styling. Very complex SVGs with external references may have issues.
